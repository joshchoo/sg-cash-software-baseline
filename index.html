Methodology

The cash test uses only the salary figure on the employer page or the job site. A monthly figure is multiplied by 12. The upper bound must be above S$240,000. A range that stops at S$240,000 does not pass.

Included work: software engineering, platform or infrastructure engineering, backend, frontend, mobile, security, reliability, distributed systems, engineering management, and internal AI or developer-platform engineering.

Excluded work: solution engineer, solutions architect, customer implementation, forward deployed engineer, and trading or HFT roles that require C++. A role is also excluded when it requires an ML-specific degree. Use of AI does not exclude a role.

MyCareersFuture Singapore is searched on every run. Official career pages are registered for the watchlist. Third-party reports stay labeled as third-party until an official page confirms them.

Accepted jobs are not deleted. A closed or unverifiable role is archived with the original URL, the salary wording, and the reason.

This run is the dataset baseline. No historical tracker records were imported.
